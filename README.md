# Blinkit Sales & Customer Analytics Dashboard

## Overview

This project is a complete end-to-end business analytics solution developed using SQL and Power BI to analyze Blinkit’s sales performance, customer behavior, inventory distribution, and outlet trends. The project focuses on transforming raw retail transaction data into actionable business insights through KPI analysis, dashboard reporting, and interactive visualizations.

The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor sales performance, identify revenue-driving categories, analyze customer purchasing behavior, and support data-driven business decisions.

---

# Business Objective

The objective of this project is to:

* Analyze customer purchasing patterns and sales trends
* Evaluate outlet performance across different outlet sizes and locations
* Identify high-performing product categories
* Monitor key business KPIs
* Generate actionable insights for business optimization

---

# Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

* Total Sales
* Average Sales
* Number of Items Sold
* Average Customer Rating

---

# Key Insights

* Analyzed 8,500+ retail transaction records using SQL and Power BI
* Identified $1.20M+ total sales across multiple outlet categories
* Tier-3 outlets generated the highest overall sales contribution
* Medium-sized outlets contributed over 42% of total sales
* Fruits, vegetables, and snack foods emerged as top-performing product categories
* Customer ratings remained consistently high across major outlet types
* Evaluated outlet establishment year trends, identifying 2018-established outlets as top-performing segments with the highest overall sales contribution.
